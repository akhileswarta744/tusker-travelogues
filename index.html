Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How do we book a College Industrial Visit (IV) package?

Simply submit the fast enquiry form or contact us on WhatsApp (+91 7909241640). We will provide customized route options, industrial company permission documentation, bus allocations, and special student group rates.

Are bus driver allowances, interstate permits, and tolls included in the price?

Yes! All our tour packages are 100% transparent with zero hidden costs. Driver beta, interstate border permits (AITP), tolls, parking, and tax are all included in your quoted package price.

What bus seating capacities are available for rental?

We operate our own fleet featuring 21, 34, 45, 49, 53, and 56 Seater AC Pushback Airbus coaches, Volvo/Scania luxury 45-seaters, 36-berth sleepers, and Force Travellers & Urbania (12 to 26 seaters).

Where is Tusker Tours & Travels located?

Our operational headquarters is in Thrissur, Kerala (Registration No: KL-02-0047002), with service pickup available across all 14 districts in Kerala.
